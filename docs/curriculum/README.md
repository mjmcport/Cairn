# Cairn curriculum

One page per step of the project, written for junior developers and for readers who are not software engineers. Each page explains what the step changed, which decisions were made and why, and what is worth learning from it.

These pages are for learning. The engineering record is elsewhere: plans in `plans/`, lessons for the people and agents building Cairn in `docs/solutions/`, and architecture decisions in `docs/adr/`.

## Pages

| Step | Page | Main lessons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 1, step 3 | [Fast first rows](phase-1-step-03-fast-first-rows.md) | Measure before optimizing; keep slow work off the main thread; check "still wanted?" after every wait; make rules machine-checkable |

Earlier steps do not have pages yet.

## What every page contains

1. The problem in plain words.
2. What changed.
3. Words you will meet, each explained in a sentence.
4. Decisions and why they were made, each ending with a lesson.
5. What the review found.
6. What was left undone.
7. Questions to think about.
8. Where to look next in the code.
